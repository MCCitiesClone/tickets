# Stats & analytics

The dashboard **home page** shows the headline numbers at a glance (last 30
days) alongside quick links to the rest of the dashboard. The dedicated
**Stats** page adds a selectable window — **7, 30, or 90 days** — a per-panel
breakdown, per-staff activity, and **CSV export**. Everything is computed from
data the bot already stores (`ticket` and `ticket_message`), so there's nothing
extra to enable.

## Summary cards

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Opened | Tickets opened within the selected range. |
| Closed | Tickets closed within the range. |
| Currently open | Tickets open right now (a live snapshot, not range-bound). |
| Avg first response | Average time from open to the first staff reply, plus how many opened tickets were answered. |
| Avg resolution | Average time from open to close, over tickets closed in the range. |

**First response** is measured as the first non-bot message from someone other
than the ticket's opener — i.e. the first time a staff member replied.

## Charts

- **Ticket volume** — opened vs. closed per day across the range.
- **Tickets by panel** — how opens break down across your panels (tickets with
  no panel are grouped as "No panel").

## Open queue by priority

A tile per [priority](/docs/tickets#priority) showing how many tickets sit at
that level **right now** (a live snapshot, like *Currently open*) with the
number opened in the selected range underneath — a quick read on whether the
escalated queue is growing.

## Staff activity

A per-staff table of tickets **closed**, tickets **claimed**, and each member's
**average resolution time**, sorted by closes. Staff are shown by their most
recent display name captured in a ticket.

## Export

Use **Export CSV** on the Stats page to download the ticket-volume series,
staff-activity table, per-panel breakdown, or per-priority breakdown for the
selected window.

**Avg rating** averages the 1–5 star [feedback](/docs/feedback) collected from
openers, over the selected window (with the number of tickets rated).

> Averages ignore tickets with no data (e.g. resolution only counts closed
> tickets; first response only counts tickets that got a reply; ratings only
> count tickets that were rated).
