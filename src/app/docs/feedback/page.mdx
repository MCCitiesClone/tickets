# Ratings & feedback

When enabled, the bot **DMs the ticket opener a 1–5 star rating prompt** as soon
as their ticket closes. The score (and an optional comment) is stored on the
transcript, shown on the transcript page, and rolled into the **average rating**
on the [Stats](/docs/stats) page.

## Enabling it

In the dashboard **Settings**, turn on **"Ask the opener for a 1–5 star rating
(DM) when a ticket closes."** It's per-server and off by default. The prompt is a
separate DM, so it works whether or not "DM the transcript link" is enabled.

## How it works

1. A ticket closes (via `/close`, a close button, a confirmed close request, or
   auto-close).
2. The opener receives a DM with five star buttons.
3. Tapping a star opens a short modal with an **optional comment**.
4. On submit, the rating + comment are saved and the prompt is marked answered.

Only the opener can answer their own prompt. If they have DMs disabled or no
longer share a server with the bot, the prompt is silently skipped — closing is
never blocked by feedback.

## Where ratings show up

- **Transcript page** — the score (and comment) appear in the ticket header.
- **Stats** — the **Avg rating** card averages all ratings in the selected
  window, alongside how many tickets were rated.
