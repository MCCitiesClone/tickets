# Tickets documentation

**Tickets** is a self-hostable Discord support-ticket bot with a companion web
dashboard. Members open private ticket channels from a button; staff claim,
manage, and close them; and every conversation is archived to a shareable
transcript. All configuration lives in the dashboard — you never edit a config
file to change how a server behaves.

The project is two processes over one PostgreSQL database:

- a **web** dashboard (Next.js) where you configure everything and sign in with
  Discord, and
- a **bot** (discord.js) that runs the slash commands and the ticket lifecycle.

See [Architecture](/docs/architecture) for how they fit together and why the bot
runs as its own process.

## What you can do

- **Channel-based tickets** — each ticket is a private channel under a category
  with per-user and staff-role permission overwrites, a per-user open limit, and
  a configurable channel-naming scheme.
- **Panels** — button messages members click to open a ticket. Every panel is
  independently configurable: category / naming / welcome overrides, support &
  mention roles, embed colour and images, access-control rules, per-user
  cooldowns, button visibility, and a disable toggle.
- **Panel forms** — attach up to five questions to a panel and collect the
  answers in a native Discord modal when the ticket opens.
- **Multi-panels** — combine several panels into one message, shown as buttons
  or a dropdown.
- **Full lifecycle** — open → claim → reply → close → transcript, with member
  add/remove, ticket rename, panel switching, close requests with an optional
  auto-close timer, and private staff notes threads.
- **Message templates** — design the welcome, claim-notice, close-DM, and
  transcript-post messages as rich embeds in the dashboard editor.
- **Transcripts** — every closed ticket is archived and viewable at a
  shareable, unguessable link; optionally DMed to the opener on close.
