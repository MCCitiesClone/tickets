# On-call staff

A support role notification only works if someone in the role happens to be
looking. **On call** narrows that to the specific people holding the pager right
now: when a ticket opens, the bot **DMs** each of them a link straight to the new
channel.

## Roster vs. on call

The roster is the pool of staff who *can* take duty; the **on call** flag marks
who's holding it right now. Keeping the two separate means a server sets its
roster up once instead of re-adding people at every handover.

- **On the roster, off call** — nothing happens on a new ticket.
- **On the roster, on call** — DMed the moment a ticket opens, with an optional
  note (e.g. "until 17:00 UTC") shown beside them on the roster.

Anyone can be on call at once, including several people — they all get the DM.

## From Discord

| Command | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/oncall list` | Staff | Shows the roster: 🟢 on call, ⚪ on the roster. |
| `/oncall claim [note]` | Staff | Takes the pager. Adds you to the roster if you're not on it. |
| `/oncall release` | Staff | Hands off. You stay on the roster. |
| `/oncall add <user>` | Manage Server | Adds someone to the roster, off call. |
| `/oncall remove <user>` | Manage Server | Removes them from the roster entirely. |

Every reply is ephemeral, so handovers don't clutter the channel. **Staff** here
means the same thing as everywhere else in the bot: a member with one of the
configured staff roles, or with **Manage Channels**.

## From the dashboard

The **On call** page manages the same roster: add staff (search members who've
replied in a ticket, or paste a Discord user ID), toggle each one on or off call,
attach a note, and remove them. It warns when the roster exists but nobody is
holding the pager.

## The notification

Each on-call member gets a DM naming the ticket, its opener, the panel it came
from, and an **Open ticket** button linking to the channel.

It's a DM rather than a mention in the ticket, for two reasons: the ticket's
welcome message is deliberately just the embed and its controls, and anything
posted in the channel would also be captured into the
[transcript](/docs/tickets#transcripts).

Best-effort, as DMs always are — a member with DMs closed simply isn't reached.
The bot records who it reached (and who it couldn't) in the
[log channel](/docs/tickets#audit-log), so a silently unreachable pager is
visible rather than invisible.

## Turning it off

**Settings → Notify on-call staff when a ticket opens** controls the DM. Off
leaves the roster in place and fully browsable — the bot just doesn't notify —
which is useful if you want on-call purely as a "who's around" board.
